Introduction to Space Exploration

Space exploration is the study and exploration of outer space using spacecraft, satellites, telescopes, and other technology. Humans have explored the Moon, sent robotic spacecraft to other planets, and studied distant stars and galaxies.

Space exploration helps scientists learn more about the universe and the origins of planets and stars. It also leads to new technologies that can be useful in everyday life.

One of the most important achievements in space exploration was the first human landing on the Moon in 1969.
